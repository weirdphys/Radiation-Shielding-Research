Kakutani / Brouwer Fixed-Point Construction from Processed Radiation Data
============================================================

Interpretation:
  Processed N003 transmission responses define a multivariate state vector along uniform log-energy.
  A two-dimensional PCA state plane is fitted with a one-step affine ordered-coordinate model.
  The clipped map g(z)=Pi_K(Az+b) is continuous and maps the compact convex box K into itself.
  Brouwer therefore guarantees a fixed point of g.
  The set-valued correspondence F(z)=K intersect closed_ball(g(z),epsilon) is the Kakutani construction used here.
  The numerical candidate satisfies the fixed-inclusion condition when ||z*-g(z*)|| <= epsilon.
  This is an ordered-coordinate reduced-model result, not a claim of a temporal fixed state in the shielding material.
